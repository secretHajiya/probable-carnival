Vyra Rewards Pro — Unity LevelPlay Ads Fix

What was fixed:
- App.js now initializes Unity LevelPlay at app startup.
- Banner ad is mounted at the bottom of the signed-in app after successful SDK initialization.
- Rewarded ad button is available on Home; 10 points are granted only from the SDK's onAdRewarded callback.
- Rewarded ad balance is saved to Firestore and local AsyncStorage.
- UnityAds.js is the single ad manager; ads.js re-exports it to avoid creating duplicate ad objects.
- app.json now includes required Android permissions, the LevelPlay Maven repository, and a local config plugin for the Play Services dependencies required by the React Native LevelPlay package.

Before building:
1. Confirm APP_KEY and the three ad unit IDs in UnityAds.js exactly match the Unity LevelPlay dashboard.
2. Create a fresh native build (EAS/Codemagic). A normal Expo Go session cannot load native LevelPlay ads.
3. If the SDK initializes but no ad fills, check LevelPlay dashboard app status, ad unit setup, mediation network adapters, and test device/network availability.

The source files were syntax-parsed, but an Android APK build and live ad fill test could not be run in this environment.
